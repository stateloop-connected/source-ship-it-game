# How to play Ship It!

## Start with one job

1. Press **Start playing**.
2. Under **New jobs**, pick a job and press **Accept job**.
3. Press **Play**.

Your starting team is already hired and assigned. They move the job through **Plan → Build → Check → Ship** automatically. You get paid when it ships.

The card above the factory tells you what to do next. When the last job ships and no customer checks are pending, time pauses so you can choose another job without spending wages while you read.

## Win your first game

In Garage days, ship **six jobs in 30 game days**. Finish with **at least $0, 45 reputation, and no unfinished jobs or repairs**.

Keep taking jobs: your team gets paid every game day. Late deliveries pay less. Bugs that reach customers may return as unpaid repairs, so leave time to finish them. Your team processes those repairs automatically.

Press **Pause** whenever you want. Nothing changes while time is paused. Opening a menu or hiding the browser tab also pauses the game.

## Add more control when you want it

Press **More controls** to reveal management tools. Press **Simple view** to hide them again. This does not restart your factory.

- **Manage a station:** click it on the floor, or choose List and select it there.
- **Add capacity:** build a station, hire a teammate, then assign them to it. Empty stations cannot work.
- **Fix slow equipment:** maintain an idle station. This costs $100 and takes four working hours.
- **Speed up a busy floor:** a coffee run costs $80 and boosts work speed by 35% for four hours. It is available every 16 hours.
- **Collect trophies:** click the star. Each factory has six to earn.

On-time deliveries in a row earn extra money, up to $200 per delivery. Late deliveries and warranty claims reset that streak.

## Try a different game

Open **Change game options** on the start screen:

- **Garage days:** the standard six-job game.
- **The Friday release:** more work and less time.
- **Open workshop:** no deadline or game-over condition.

You can also choose a studio: Bootstrappers have lower wages, Craft Club improves planning and review, and Night Owls ship faster. The seed identifies the starting challenge; leave it alone unless you want to replay or share a particular one.

## Keep or share your factory

Progress saves in this browser. Use **Game menu → Export save** to keep a portable copy, then **Import save** to restore it. Starting fresh replaces the browser autosave.

**Share challenge** copies the seed, scenario, and studio—not your current progress. Results are repeatable with the same game version and choices. Scores are local and unverified.

Older saves import with their factory intact. Version-1 saves use the Original crew studio and begin a fresh trophy collection.

## Optional controls

Space plays or pauses. N advances one hour while paused. B opens the station catalog. Escape cancels placement or closes a menu. Tab and Enter work with buttons; the List view also supports station selection and placement.

Sound is off by default. The game supports reduced motion. Both settings are in the game menu.
