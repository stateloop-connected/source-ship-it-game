# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — The Good Work update

- Three studio specialties: Bootstrappers, Craft Club, and Night Owls.
- On-time shipping streaks with cash bonuses, reset by late deliveries or warranty claims.
- A coffee run ability: spend $80 for four hours of 35% extra throughput, available every 16 hours.
- Six trophies per factory, with a shelf that shows earned achievements and the next goals.
- Delivery celebrations, contextual crew banter, and reduced-motion support.
- Studio-aware challenge links and a richer factory report.
- Version-1 save migration preserves the existing factory under the Original crew specialty. New streaks and trophies begin at zero; future play uses the updated rules.
- A gameplay GIF, fresh screenshots, studio challenge links, and an expanded player guide.

## 1.0.0 — Open for business

The original release: three scenarios, four production stages, developers and agents, workstation placement and upgrades, defects and warranty repairs, seeded challenges, autosaves, portable saves, and a complete campaign report.
